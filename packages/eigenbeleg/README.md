# @le-space/eigenbeleg

The Eigenbeleg and the Kontoauszug of Le Space's tools – [Belege](https://github.com/Le-Space/belege) and the [Eigenbeleg](https://github.com/Le-Space/eigenbeleg-agent) API and web UI: what they say, drawn as PDF/A-2b ([`@le-space/pdf`](../pdf), text in any script) and written as CSV. Runs in a browser and in Node; the same document is the same file in both. The documents are German, for German bookkeeping.

An Eigenbeleg is a receipt the payer writes for a payment that has no receipt from the other side. It is never an invoice, and never one written for somebody else.

- **A period's Eigenbeleg** (Eigenbeleg's): `valueEntries(entries, rates)` values [`@le-space/ledger`](../ledger) entries at the day's rate (`rates.rateFor(asset, day)`, e.g. from [`@le-space/rates`](../rates); `fixedRates` for tests; Aleph credits at their USD price per credit). `buildDocument({ payer, chain, network, period, entries, number, includeKinds, layout })` puts the payments to the network on it (fees, storage deposits; transfers only when included) and says of every other movement why not; `layout: 'collective'` (one Sammel-Eigenbeleg) or `'single'` (one per transaction, numbered up from the first, `nthNumber`). `eigenbelegPdf(doc, { logo })`, `eigenbelegCsv(doc)` (semicolons, decimal commas, BOM; formulas defused).
- **The Kontoauszug** (Eigenbeleg's): `buildStatement({ payer, account, chain, period, entries, opening, closing, method, belege })` – opening balance, each booking with debit, credit and running balance, closing balance checked against the chain (`reconciled(difference)`: exactly, or but for dust below the twelve decimals the documents print, `isDust`, such as NEAR's 1 yocto deposit of a token transfer); Aleph credits and a payer's balance in Filecoin Pay (chain `filecoin-pay`, the entries of `@le-space/chains/filecoin-pay`'s `filecoinPayEntries`) as a monthly **Verbrauchsauszug**, each with its own note (`USAGE_NOTES`). `statementPdf(st, { logo, chart })`. Above each list of bookings stands a small diagram ([#889](https://github.com/Le-Space/belege/issues/889)), drawn as vectors from [`@le-space/charts`](../charts): the balance over the period on a Kontoauszug, the consumption per resource on a Verbrauchsauszug, per day (per month where the period is longer than a quarter), in the asset's own unit; `chart: false` leaves it out. `statementChart(st, section)` gives its series and its scene, for an API's answer or `svg(scene)`; `drawScene(page, scene, { x, top, fonts })` draws any scene on a PDF page. `logo: true` draws the chain's mark, small, beside the title (NEAR, Akash, Filecoin, Aleph; from `@le-space/pdf`'s `LOGOS`, sources and terms in its `assets/README.md`); off unless asked for.
- **Another currency than the euro** ([eigenbeleg-agent#72](https://github.com/Le-Space/eigenbeleg-agent/issues/72)): `valueEntries(entries, rates, { currency })`, `buildDocument({ …, currency })` and `buildStatement({ …, currency })` take the document's currency, EUR when none is given – `DOCUMENT_CURRENCIES`: EUR, USD, CHF, GBP, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, CZK, HUF, RON, ISK, TRY (through the ECB's reference rate) and RUB (the Bank of Russia's official rate). The rates handed in are in that currency and say so (`{ rate, source, date, currency }`; `@le-space/rates` `rate(asset, date, { currency })`), `source` naming both legs; a value in one currency under a head in another is refused. The documents then say _Kurs CHF_, _Wert CHF_, amounts as `1.234,56 CHF`, sums rounded to the currency's own decimals (ISK: none), and how the value came about (`conversionText`, `usageNote`); Aleph credits in USD are Aleph's own USD price, without a rate. The fields named `eur…` (`entry.eur`, `totals.eur`, `eurOut` …) keep their names and hold the value in the document's currency, with `currency` beside them. An EUR document is the same file as before. `bookingPdf` is Belege's own booking and stays in euro.
- **The Eigenbeleg of one booking** (Belege's, `@le-space/eigenbeleg/booking`, `/booking-pdf`): `bookingPdf(doc)` for a `BookingEigenbeleg` – a bank booking or a crypto payment, with an own wallet's transaction in full (whole hash, both addresses, every movement with the gas, the explorer link as a QR code). The booking's context is the caller's to gather. `nextSelfNumber(receipts, year)` – the range EB-YYYY-NNN.
- **A statement Eigenbeleg** (Belege's Aleph, Akash and travel statements, `@le-space/eigenbeleg/statement-layout`): `statementLayout({ title, created, continuation })` → `head`, `field`, `rule`, `heading`, `table` (the header repeats on a new page), `signature`, `save`.

Every PDF has Eigenbeleg's look (`layout.js`, [#951](https://github.com/Le-Space/belege/issues/951)): A4 with 48 pt margins, the title first and a strong rule under the head, small grey labels, rules in two weights and no fills or boxes, a total between two rules, colour for a warning only, page numbers at the foot, a line to sign. The sizes, colours and rule weights are tokens (`TYPE`, `INK`, `GREY`, `RULE`, `WARN`, `STROKE`); nothing is set outside them. `creator` names the program in the metadata (`eigenbeleg` for the period's documents, `Belege` for a booking's and a statement's unless given).

## The issuer's own logo

An Eigenbeleg is issued by the payer, so the logo that belongs on it is the payer's ([#906](https://github.com/Le-Space/belege/issues/906)). `eigenbelegPdf(doc, { issuerLogo })`, `bookingPdf(doc, { issuerLogo })` and `statementLayout({ …, issuerLogo })` take the bytes of a PNG or JPEG file and draw it large, as a letterhead: top right, fitted into about 70 × 25 mm (`ISSUER_LOGO_BOX`, 200 × 70 pt) with its proportions kept, the text below it and not above 45 mm from the top edge (`LETTERHEAD`, the head zone of DIN 5008 form B). Without a logo the page is as before. The chain's mark (`logo: true`) is the network's, not the issuer's: on an Eigenbeleg it stays very small beside the title (`MARK_SIZE.small`); on a Kontoauszug or Verbrauchsauszug, which shows what the account did and is not the issuer's own receipt, it is larger (`MARK_SIZE.large`) and the statement carries no issuer's logo. The package ships no such picture and fetches none: the caller hands it in.

The file goes through [`@le-space/pdf`](../pdf)'s `pdfImage` first: at most 512 kB and 2048 pixels a side, no wider than six times its height and no taller than twice its width (`IMAGE_LIMITS`); a PNG is laid on white and written again without transparency, text chunks or a colour profile, a JPEG loses EXIF, comments and its other application segments. SVG is refused, as is a JPEG in CMYK. A file that does not pass is an `ImageError` with a sentence for the person, never a document without its logo.

```bash
pnpm --filter @le-space/eigenbeleg test           # Node, and every document byte for byte in Chromium
pnpm --filter @le-space/eigenbeleg pdfa-samples out/   # one of each, for veraPDF (CI runs it)
```

The model, the CSV, the Kontoauszug and the PDF layout came from Eigenbeleg's `packages/eigenbeleg` at e6cde90; the booking's Eigenbeleg and the statement layout from Belege's `app/src/lib/receipts/eigenbeleg*.js` and `app/src/lib/pdf/statement-layout.js` (moved with their history).
